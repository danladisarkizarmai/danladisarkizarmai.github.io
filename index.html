Biography

Danladi Sarki Zarmai, known by the traditional title Wazirin Zumba, is a Nigerian businessman and retired safety professional based in Shiroro, Niger State. His professional experience includes service in the Internal Facility Safety Unit at North South Power Company Limited. He began working in 1992 and retired in 2014.

He also has business interests in contracting and retail. His educational background includes a National Diploma (ND) and a Higher National Diploma (HND) in Business Administration from Niger State Polytechnic, Zungeru.
